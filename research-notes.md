## Session 1 — Understanding the CFTR F508del Mutation

### 1. Normal function of CFTR
The CFTR gene contains instructions for making a protein that acts as a chloride channel. It helps regulate the movement of chloride ions and water across epithelial cell membranes.

### 2. What is F508del?
F508del is a mutation in which three DNA nucleotides (CTT) are deleted from the CFTR coding sequence. This removes the amino acid phenylalanine at position 508 of the CFTR protein.

### 3. How does the mutation affect the protein?
The deletion disrupts the folding and processing of the CFTR protein. Much of the faulty protein does not reach the cell membrane, reducing chloride transport.

### 4. What were the researchers trying to achieve?
The researchers investigated whether CRISPR-Cas9 could restore the missing DNA sequence using a DNA repair template, allowing cells to produce a more functional CFTR protein.

### Session 1 conclusion
I learned that a deletion of just three DNA letters can affect protein structure and contribute to a genetic disease. I also learned how CRISPR-Cas9 may be used to attempt to repair this mutation.

## Session 2 — Reproducing the CRISPR Experimental Design

### Figure 1: sgRNAs and ssODNs for the CFTR locus

The researchers designed several CRISPR-Cas9 targeting combinations to correct the F508del mutation.

**1. Guide RNAs (sgRNAs)**

Three guide RNAs were investigated:
- sp_sg#1
- sp_sg#2
- sa_sg#1

These guide RNAs direct Cas9 to selected regions around or upstream of the mutation.

**2. Cas9 enzymes**

The researchers investigated two Cas9 systems:
- SpCas9, from Streptococcus pyogenes.
- SaCas9, from Staphylococcus aureus.

**3. DNA repair templates (ssODNs)**

Three single-stranded DNA repair templates were designed:
- sp_ssODN#1
- sp_ssODN#2
- sa_ssODN#1

These templates contain the sequence intended to restore the missing DNA letters.

**4. Expected DNA repair**

Cas9 creates a double-strand break at the selected target. The researchers supply a repair template that the cell may use through homology-directed repair (HDR).

**5. How success was measured**

The researchers sequenced the DNA after editing to determine whether the F508del mutation had been corrected.

### My interpretation

Figure 1 shows that the researchers tested multiple guide RNA and repair-template combinations rather than relying on a single CRISPR design. Their objective was to identify combinations capable of accurately restoring the missing DNA sequence.
