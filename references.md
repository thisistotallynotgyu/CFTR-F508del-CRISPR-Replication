# References

## Main research paper

Smirnikhina, S. A., et al. (2020). *P.F508del editing in cells from cystic fibrosis patients*. **PLOS ONE**.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0242094
- Full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7657551/

## How this source was used

The paper was the main source for this replication project. I used it to understand the CFTR F508del mutation, identify the CRISPR-Cas9 editing strategies shown in Figure 1, record the reported HDR results, and discuss the study's limitations.

**Note:** The experimental results and figures discussed in this project are based on the published paper. I did not perform laboratory gene editing.
